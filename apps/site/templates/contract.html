Contract Lab

Design a weather contract and see what it would pay.

Choose a payoff shape, a strike and a payout per degree day for the selected county and month. The atlas prices the contract on 2,000 simulated seasons, shows how often it would pay and by how much, and how much of that risk the selected listed station could hedge.

Three separate numbers

The expected payout, any assumed risk-transfer loading and an observed market price are always reported apart. This release assumes no loading and reproduces no market prices, so nothing here is a quote.

Research and education only

Everything here is a model estimate built from public NOAA temperature records. Nothing is an executable quote, an offer, insurance, investment advice, or a promise of hedge performance. The project is not affiliated with CME Group or Speedwell/Xweather; station names identify public weather stations and no exchange price data are reproduced.
