Portfolio Lab

Build a protection book and see what it costs.

Start from an example book or your own holdings, choose which station contracts may be used, and let the optimiser find the cheapest protection against 2,000 simulated seasons. Risk is measured as ES90, the average loss in the worst 10% of those seasons. Everything runs and stays in your browser.

What to expect

If no feasible hedge exists under your rules, the lab says so rather than quietly recommending nothing. Costs are illustrative model premiums, not market quotes.

Research and education only

Everything here is a model estimate built from public NOAA temperature records. Nothing is an executable quote, an offer, insurance, investment advice, or a promise of hedge performance. The project is not affiliated with CME Group or Speedwell/Xweather; station names identify public weather stations and no exchange price data are reproduced.
