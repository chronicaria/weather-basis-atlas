Where does a weather index actually travel?

Every U.S. county has a temperature index, but only thirteen cities have listed weather contracts. This atlas shows how well the best listed station would have hedged each county's heating and cooling degree days, month by month, across 45 years of matched history.

Everything here is a model estimate built from public NOAA temperature records. Nothing is an executable quote, an offer, insurance, investment advice, or a promise of hedge performance.
