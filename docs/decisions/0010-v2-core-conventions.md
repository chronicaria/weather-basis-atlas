# ADR 0010: V2 scientific and execution conventions

Accepted 2026-09-05 before revised national results. This implements managerial defaults D01–D20 in the controlling V2 manual.

Python 3.12/uv/NumPy/SciPy/Arrow/Jinja and modular ES modules/D3 remain the stack. Frozen strict dataclasses define the schema. GitHub Pages hosts the static public core under the existing chronicaria/weather-basis-atlas identity. No accounts or remote customer uploads.

V2 effectiveness is one minus uncentered residual SSE divided by centered target SST on exactly paired common seasons. Degenerate target support is unavailable with a reason. Frozen pre-outcome eligibility and prior scores choose stations; realized missingness only affects later scoreability. Current decisions, historical policies, evaluations, and contract years are different identities.

Positive loss harms the customer; signed long cash flow reduces that loss; premiums/fees/funding enter once. USD only, terminal values at zero discount in the illustrative default. Weighted fractional-tail ES90 is default; historical tail support is displayed separately from predictive Monte Carlo error. Physical expected payouts, assumed loads, and absent market observations are different components.

Common scenario IDs span locations and months; sorted marginals cannot enter joint arithmetic. Weather/index paths and scenario sample identity are scientific. Workers, cache paths, telemetry and timestamps are execution metadata. Browser inputs use one release-wide sample of 2,000 paths; offline adjudication initially uses 10,000.

Simple baseline ladder: no hedge, nearest eligible, training-only single, shrinkage/simple policy, sparse basket. Reporting sensitivity bands are 0, 0.02 and 0.05 HE, frozen managerial anchors rather than universal economic thresholds. Sample dollar exposures and costs are illustrative, not estimated business economics. Unsupported market/forecast/settlement modes remain unavailable.

The 2023–2025 outcomes have been consumed by development; historical results are exploratory. Dependent origin/geographic diagnostic signs cannot support an independent-binomial claim. A next-station pilot follows core and may validly be negative or unsupported.

One global compute owner begins with one worker then measures two; four require memory evidence. V1 artifacts and old links remain explicitly identified. Runtime targets and model outperformance are measured outcomes; correctness, actual custom optimization and release identity remain hard requirements.
